\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$ / $J$ use a procedural no-cliff sibling for seams and an engine-identity prior for the mid-cycle body. They do not measure perceptual quality. Formal MOS/MUSHRA panels are out of scope; we ship spectrograms and downloadable WAVs for informal audition only.
  \item PESQ and STOI are excluded: primary cycles are non-speech wavetable tiles. Speech metrics on sine{+}cliff families would be a domain mismatch. MUSHRA was not run.
  \item No LibriSpeech / MUSDB. Wavetable-native realism uses ReelSynth Factory+FX exports (${\approx}1{,}280$) and AKWF CC0 single-cycle WAVs (${\approx}1{,}280$). Procedural stand-ins are tertiary smoke only.
  \item HP sensitivity (paragraph~\ref{para:hp-sensitivity}) is a short one-at-a-time $\pm 50\%$ probe, not a full re-search of Table~\ref{tab:hyperparams} defaults.
  \item Sci/eng transfer (Section~\ref{sec:transfer-main}) is classical-board only where deep models did not run. Cycle-GAN, BeatDiff, Paderborn deep, real KIT/PMU streams, and formal MOS remain not executed (Table~\ref{tab:transfer-sota-status}).
  \item Lightweight SSM seq models are deferred; LSTM remains the supervised seq ceiling.
  \item On exported Factory+FX / Rust tiles the meta favorite can trail no-bake or FIR (domain shift). We do not claim universal factory superiority.
  \item Search scores Python \texttt{make\_batch} sine{+}cliff draws. The Rust \texttt{sound\_bench} export of 20 family tiles is secondary. The Python multi-family matrix is primary.
  \item Per-trial architecture width is capped for search throughput. Full Demucs-/diffusion-scale stacks were screened out. The champ gate vs Noise2Noise may not be met at every budget; we report the measured freeze honestly.
  \item We do not claim wrap-closure or hybrid-search convergence theorems. Endpoint-pin shows wrap-jump can be zeroed while residual falls.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future work}
\label{sec:outlook}

A follow-up can treat \emph{which sounds fail} as the primary object: stratified residual heatmaps over the ten procedural families, cliff-size conditioning, and family-specialist or mixture-of-experts denoisers selected by the same RL--GA outer loop.
That paper would cite the present mean-$R$ hybrid protocol as baseline and ask whether beating $R>0.999$ is mainly an architecture problem or a coverage problem over hard families.
