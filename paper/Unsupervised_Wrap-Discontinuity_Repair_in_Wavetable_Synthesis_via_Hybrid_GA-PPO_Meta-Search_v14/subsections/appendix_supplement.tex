\subsection{Matched-budget outer-loop comparison}
\label{sec:meta-approaches-results}

Under a matched $5{,}000$-evaluation budget with LSTM and xLSTM in the searchable bake vocabulary, how do Random NAS, Cont.\ CMA-ES, Arch REINFORCE, Aging evolution, and TPE Bayes NAS compare to Ours (hybrid GA+PPO)?
Table~\ref{tab:meta-approaches} reports the completed Deferred~D1 matched re-search under locked $R_{\mathrm{blend}}{+}J$
(mean$\pm$std over three search seeds; artifacts \texttt{meta\_approach\_compare\_v13\_rblend/}).
Ours leads ($R_{\mathrm{blend}}{=}0.9748{\pm}0.0018$; mean wall ${\approx}8.4$\,h).
Aging evolution is second ($0.9635{\pm}0.0049$); Arch REINFORCE is highest-variance ($0.9599{\pm}0.0116$).
Only seed~\texttt{1902771841} hybrid clears the frozen Noise2Noise gate under the short-FitCell D1 protocol ($R_{\mathrm{blend}}{\approx}0.9766$ vs gate ${\approx}0.9750$).
Hybrid live champions stay compact (${\approx}24$k$\pm$2k params across seeds) despite mid-search graphs that swing from ${\approx}15$k to ${\approx}370$k.
Because hybrid already dominated this matched bake-off, the next campaign drops the five losing families and runs hybrid-only FitCell-to-convergence (Section~\ref{sec:v14-converge}, Table~\ref{tab:v14-hybrid-converge}).
Noise2Noise is not an outer-loop method here (see Tables~\ref{tab:n2n-vs-ours} and~\ref{tab:sota-main}).
Learning-curve and bar figures use seed~\texttt{1902771841} trajectories under the same locked objective.
