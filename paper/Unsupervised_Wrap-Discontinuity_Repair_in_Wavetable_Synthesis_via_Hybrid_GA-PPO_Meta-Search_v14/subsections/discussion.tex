\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Main story under $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$.}
The locked protocol does not crown the hybrid champion over Noise2Noise on holdout
($0.9708{\pm}0.0014$ vs $0.9767{\pm}0.0015$ over five seeds).
Dual Cosine on that board sits near $0.542{\pm}0.038$.
The operative claim is narrower.
Compact searchable repair operators outperform classical fades, stay competitive with Noise2Noise on multi-family means (mean win fraction $0.47$), and hold on hard cliffs where Dual Cosine collapses
(Table~\ref{tab:n2n-vs-ours}, Section~\ref{sec:cliff-strata}).
Favorite $\Theta$ on the v10.1 freeze is larger than SeamN2N (${\approx}121$k vs ${\approx}53.5$k) and still trails N2N on holdout $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$
(Table~\ref{tab:param-fairness}).
Whole-curve numbers near $R{\approx}0.991$ come from superseded freezes.
They are not current results under the locked protocol (Limitations).

\paragraph{Which families stay hard.}
Across large procedural benches, wrap error is uneven.
Families such as \texttt{nonlinear}, \texttt{combo}, \texttt{triple\_mix}, and \texttt{extreme\_overlay} dominate the residual budget.
\texttt{harmonic\_fft} and \texttt{am\_fm} are easier.
Mean scores hide that structure.

\paragraph{Search near the ceiling.}
Under near-ceiling residuals, further gains are small in absolute units and sensitive to reward shaping and branch weights.
Learning curves (Figure~\ref{fig:search-learning-curve}) show Dual Cosine cleared early under both the superseded prolonged-$R$ $5$k bake-off and the v10.1 $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$ hybrid run, with a later plateau below the Noise2Noise gate under short FitCell.
Plateau adaptation (deeper operators, denser mixture-of-experts graphs, higher GA/PBT weight) is the operational response inside one campaign.
Matched-$5$k outer-loop bake-offs under locked $R_{\mathrm{blend}}{+}J$ are complete across three search seeds (Appendix~\ref{app:supplement}, Table~\ref{tab:meta-approaches}): hybrid leads, which is why the converge follow-up spends the GPU on hybrid alone (Section~\ref{sec:v14-converge}).
Training candidates to residual plateau rather than a $16$--$48$-step FitCell cap is what lets the completed converge seed clear the Noise2Noise gate ($R_{\mathrm{blend}}{\approx}0.9912$).

\paragraph{Evidence beyond static tiles.}
Vibrato spectrograms and downloadable hear WAVs support informal inspection only
(Sections~\ref{sec:vibrato-eval}--\ref{sec:hear-samples}; $N_{\mathrm{listeners}}{=}0$, no MOS/MUSHRA).
Sci/eng wrap transfer reports locked $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$ on seven boards as a wrap-protocol stress test with per-domain seam analogies
(Section~\ref{sec:transfer-main}, Table~\ref{tab:transfer-main}).
It is not a claim that an audio bake transfers as bearing-fault or ECG diagnosis SOTA.
Perceptual and scope limits are collected in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.
