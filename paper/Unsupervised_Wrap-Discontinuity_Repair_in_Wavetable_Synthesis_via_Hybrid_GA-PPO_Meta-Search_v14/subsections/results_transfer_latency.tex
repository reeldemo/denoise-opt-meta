\paragraph{Transfer inference latency.}
Table~\ref{tab:transfer-latency} reports wall-clock inference on transfer holdout batches
(device=\texttt{cuda}, batch=64, warmup=20, timed=100, includes residual scoring).
These timings pair with the prolonged-$R$ transfer board (Table~\ref{tab:transfer-main}).
N2N latency uses the wavetable \texttt{n2n\_corrupt\_corrupt} checkpoint zero-shot.
Domain-trained N2N quality scores are in Table~\ref{tab:transfer-main}.
