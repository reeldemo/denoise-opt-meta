\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

We group prior work by mechanism.
\textbf{Used} citations are anchors for residual-scored RL+GA meta-search.
\textbf{Screened} citations were checked as contrast and are not dependencies.

\paragraph{Discontinuity control and modular DSP.}
BLIT/BLEP~\cite{stilson1996}, PolyBLEP~\cite{nam2009polyblep}, and BLAMP~\cite{esqueda2016blamp} state the wrap and corner problem for trivial oscillators.
We score cycle-local approximations beside Dual Cosine (Section~\ref{sec:va-seam-baselines}).
DDSP frames DSP modules as composable blocks~\cite{engel2020}.
We reuse that framing for small bake/FIR/MLP seam operators at cycle scale, rather than end-to-end vocoders.

\paragraph{Label-free restoration.}
Noise2Noise learns restorers from corrupted observations alone~\cite{lehtinen2018,kashyap2021n2n}.
Mixture-invariant training ranks unsupervised systems without stem labels~\cite{wisdom2020}.
We train a corrupt-to-corrupt seam CNN on the same generator as the primary neural baseline (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
Full Demucs / DiffWave stacks are screened: domain and bake-budget mismatch~\cite{dfossez2019,kong2021}.

\paragraph{Compact waveform operators.}
Wave-U-Net, Conv-TasNet, dual-path, and attention audio models supply block priors we shrink to bake width~\cite{stoller2018,luo2019,luo2020dprnn,subakan2021,ronneberger2015,he2016resnet,vaswani2017}.
They supply block priors rather than serving as frozen full-scale separators inside every meta trial.

\paragraph{Search and hybrids.}
NAS taxonomies, RL controllers, progressive and latency-aware NAS, aging evolution, PBT, CMA-ES, and evolution-guided policy gradients motivate the hybrid outer loop~\cite{elsken2019,zoph2017nas,pham2018,liu2018pnas,tan2019mnasnet,real2019,jaderberg2017,hansen2016cmaes,khadka2018,schulman2017}.
MoE soft gates motivate routing among small heterogeneous operators under one residual score~\cite{shazeer2017}.
Matched outer-loop bake-offs (Random, CMA-ES, REINFORCE, aging evolution, TPE vs hybrid) sit in Appendix~\ref{app:supplement}.

\paragraph{Used vs screened (short).}
Used: VA discontinuity~\cite{stilson1996,esqueda2016blamp,nam2009polyblep}; unsupervised restoration~\cite{lehtinen2018,kashyap2021n2n,wisdom2020}; DDSP~\cite{engel2020}; compact waveform operators as above; NAS/RL/PPO/PBT/CMA-ES/ERL/MoE as above.
Screened as contrast only: MAML~\cite{finn2017}, DARTS~\cite{liu2019}, Demucs-scale enhancers~\cite{dfossez2019,dfossez2021}, DiffWave~\cite{kong2021}, SEGAN-style full GAN loops~\cite{pascual2017}.
Claims stay inside cycle-local seam restoration under a declared compute budget.
