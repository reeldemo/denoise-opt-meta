\paragraph{Hyperparameter sensitivity.}
\label{para:hp-sensitivity}
How sensitive is hybrid champion $R$ to $\pm 50\%$ shifts of Table~\ref{tab:hyperparams} knobs?
We run a \textbf{one-at-a-time sensitivity probe} (not a full 5,000-iteration re-search).
Each config uses $500$ outer iterations, seed \texttt{1902771841}, and sine{+}cliff FitCell matched to the meta-compare protocol (batch $48$, fit steps $24$).
Perturbed knobs: population size $n$, PPO clip $\epsilon$, fit Adam learning rate, GA second-mutate rate, and PPO entropy coefficient.
Table~\ref{tab:hp-sensitivity} and Figure~\ref{fig:hp-sensitivity} report champion absolute $R$ versus the Table-2 default (default champion $R\approx 0.9888$). The largest $|\Delta R|$ among completed arms is $\approx 0.0123$ for a 50\% lower learning rate.
All $11/11$ arms completed under this budget.

This is local sensitivity evidence under the stated budget and seed.
The 5,000-evaluation matched-budget ranking (Table~\ref{tab:meta-approaches}) remains the primary approach comparison.
We do \emph{not} claim that Table-2 defaults are globally optimal.
We only report that $\pm 50\%$ one-at-a-time shifts leave champion $R$ within a small band relative to the DualCosine gap ($\approx 0.17$ in the 5,000-evaluation comparison).
