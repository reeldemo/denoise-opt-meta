\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Main story under $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$.}
The locked protocol does not crown the hybrid champion over Noise2Noise on the holdout
($0.9697$ vs $0.9750$).
Dual Cosine on that board sits near $0.541$.
The operative claim is narrower.
Compact searchable repair operators outperform classical fades, stay competitive with Noise2Noise on multi-family means ($13/20$ wins), and hold on hard cliffs where Dual Cosine collapses
(Table~\ref{tab:n2n-vs-ours}, Section~\ref{sec:cliff-strata}).
Favorite $\Theta$ on the v10.1 freeze is larger than SeamN2N (${\approx}121$k vs ${\approx}53.5$k) and still trails N2N on holdout $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$
(Table~\ref{tab:param-fairness}).
Whole-curve numbers near $R{\approx}0.991$ come from superseded freezes.
They are not current results under the locked protocol (Limitations).

\paragraph{Which families stay hard.}
Across large procedural benches, wrap error is uneven.
Families such as \texttt{nonlinear}, \texttt{combo}, \texttt{triple\_mix}, and \texttt{extreme\_overlay} dominate the residual budget.
\texttt{harmonic\_fft} and \texttt{am\_fm} are easier.
Mean scores hide that structure.

\paragraph{Search near the ceiling.}
Under near-ceiling residuals, further gains are small in absolute units and sensitive to reward shaping and branch weights.
Learning curves (Figure~\ref{fig:search-learning-curve}) show Dual Cosine cleared early under both the superseded prolonged-$R$ $5$k bake-off and the v10.1 $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$ hybrid run, with a later plateau below the Noise2Noise gate.
Plateau adaptation (deeper operators, denser mixture-of-experts graphs, higher GA/PBT weight) is the operational response inside one campaign.
Matched outer-loop bake-offs and HP probes sit in Appendix~\ref{app:supplement} (still prolonged $R$ until D1).

\paragraph{Evidence beyond static tiles.}
Vibrato spectrograms and downloadable hear WAVs support informal inspection (Sections~\ref{sec:vibrato-eval}--\ref{sec:hear-samples}).
Sci/eng wrap transfer is a wrap-protocol stress test with per-domain seam analogies (Section~\ref{sec:transfer-main}).
