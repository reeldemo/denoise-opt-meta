\paragraph{Takeaways.}
Under prolonged $R$, Ours leads every classical-board column in Table~\ref{tab:transfer-main},
including Dual Cosine and SeamN2N on every domain.
SeamN2N beats Dual Cosine everywhere and sits near no-bake on CWRU/MFPT/Paderborn, but trails Ours by a wide margin
on ECG and synth CNC ($R{\approx}0.56$--$0.65$ and $0.48$ vs Ours ${>}0.93$).
On MIT-BIH and PTB-XL, endpoint-pin remains a strong classical ECG row above SeamN2N; Ours still leads both.
Author Operational Cycle-GAN on the same ECG wrap boards collapses under OOD wrap-$R$
($R{\approx}0.07$--$0.16$)---clinical ECG restore is not the wrap residual objective, but the author stack
is now a Table~\ref{tab:transfer-main} row rather than a deferred claim.
Synthetic CNC shows the largest classical collapse with Ours near $0.992$; SeamN2N only recovers to $0.4833$.
Synth PMU is the strongest SeamN2N row ($R{=}0.9789$) but still below Ours ($0.9915$).
Paderborn K001 wrap board shows Ours $R{=}0.9270$ vs SeamN2N $0.8387$ and DualCosine $0.4710$;
the published Al~Firdausi CNN runs as a native fault classifier on K001 (healthy$\to$Normal rate $1.0$)
and is not forced into wrap-$R$ (Table~\ref{tab:transfer-sota-status}).
BeatDiff author priors remain unavailable without HF login or a complete Drive Orbax download.
Latency timings in Appendix~\ref{app:supplement} remain wavetable-checkpoint zero-shot;
primary claim remains cycle-local wavetable seam restoration.
