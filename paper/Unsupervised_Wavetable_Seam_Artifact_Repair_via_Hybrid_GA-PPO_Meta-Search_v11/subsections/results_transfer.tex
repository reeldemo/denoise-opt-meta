\paragraph{Takeaways.}
Under prolonged $R$, Ours leads every classical-board column in Table~\ref{tab:transfer-main},
including Dual Cosine and domain-trained Noise2Noise on every domain.
Domain N2N beats Dual Cosine everywhere and sits near no-bake on CWRU/MFPT/Paderborn, but trails Ours by a wide margin
on ECG and synth CNC ($R{\approx}0.56$--$0.65$ and $0.48$ vs Ours ${>}0.93$).
On MIT-BIH and PTB-XL, endpoint-pin remains a strong classical ECG row above domain N2N; Ours still leads both.
Synthetic CNC shows the largest classical collapse with Ours near $0.992$; domain N2N only recovers to $0.4833$.
Synth PMU is the strongest domain-N2N row ($R{=}0.9789$) but still below Ours ($0.9915$).
Paderborn K001 (Table~\ref{tab:transfer-main}) shows Ours $R{=}0.9270$ vs domain N2N $0.8387$ and DualCosine $0.4710$
(smoke $20$-iter run was $0.8932$ and is not used as the protocol number).
An Operational Cycle-GAN checkpoint loads and scores under wrap-$R$, but the OOD probe collapses
($R{\approx}0.07$--$0.15$; Table~\ref{tab:transfer-sota-status})---clinical ECG restore is not the wrap residual objective.
Latency timings in Appendix~\ref{app:supplement} remain wavetable-checkpoint zero-shot;
primary claim remains cycle-local wavetable seam restoration.
