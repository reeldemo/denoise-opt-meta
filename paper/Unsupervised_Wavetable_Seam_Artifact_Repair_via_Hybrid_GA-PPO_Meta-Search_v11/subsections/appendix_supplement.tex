\subsection{Matched-budget outer-loop comparison}
\label{sec:meta-approaches-results}

Under a matched $5{,}000$-evaluation budget with LSTM and xLSTM in the searchable bake vocabulary, how do Random NAS, Cont.\ CMA-ES, Arch REINFORCE, Aging evolution, and TPE Bayes NAS compare to Ours (hybrid GA+PPO)?
Table~\ref{tab:meta-approaches} reports a \textbf{cheap} $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$ re-fit of each frozen champion architecture/HP from that historical search
(not a full overnight re-search; outer loops still maximized prolonged $R$).
Wall hours are the original 5k-search walls.
Full matched re-search under $R_{\mathrm{blend}}{+}J$ remains Deferred D1
(\texttt{.venv\_gpu/Scripts/python.exe scripts/bench\_meta\_approaches\_5k.py --iters 5000 --device cuda}, write to a new \texttt{meta\_approach\_compare\_v11\_rblend/} tree).
Only the $1{,}200$-iteration hybrid freeze under $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$ is locked as a searched result (Section~\ref{sec:v10-freeze}).
Noise2Noise is not an outer-loop method here (see Tables~\ref{tab:n2n-vs-ours} and~\ref{tab:sota-main}).
On the one-shot re-fit board, Ours still leads ($R_{\mathrm{blend}}{\approx}0.97484$, $\Delta R{\approx}{+}0.436$ vs Dual Cosine ${\approx}0.539$, original wall ${\approx}8.46$\,h). Aging evolution is second ($R_{\mathrm{blend}}{\approx}0.96519$).
Neither Ours nor Aging evolution selected an LSTM block in the champion graph.
